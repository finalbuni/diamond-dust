This evening, Erich was struck and humiliated by the Master.

Josef's father had, until just a few months ago, been one of the principal voices urging joint intervention with Prussia to prevent Denmark from annexing Schleswig and Holstein. He had enjoyed the Emperor's trust and wielded considerable influence within Austria.
