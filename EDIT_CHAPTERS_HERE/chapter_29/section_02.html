He picked up the black leather case resting beside his everyday belongings — the books he was currently reading, his wallet, wristwatch, keys, and the framed picture he'd received from Suki Kim — and lay back down.

Whenever Liu's left hand came briefly into view on their video calls, Yeehyeon's eyes went to the ring. He'd never said anything about it, but there was a strange satisfaction in seeing the ring glint on those long, straight fingers. It satisfied a desire to possess Liu, to have him all to himself, that he hadn't even known he had.

No — he had been vaguely aware of these desires regarding Liu before. It was simply that Liu had always provided more than enough to satisfy them without ever needing to be asked or pressured, so there had been no opportunity to express them.

Perhaps Liu and he were only just now officially starting a romantic relationship. That didn't mean the depth of their feelings was at the beginning stage.

The love that had matured within him and the realm of dating — the method for expressing and exchanging that love — could seem to coincide yet function separately. It was strange that while their very existence was already deeply rooted in each other's lives, the form of their romance felt similar to that of couples just beginning their relationship.

It wasn't the imbalance where one speeds ahead, focusing so intensely on the other that everything else falls away. It wasn't the blind anxiety of wanting to leap straight into deep connection, skipping all the in-between stages. It was a relationship in which he wanted to know the small details of everyday life, expressed his jealousy carefully, and willingly held himself back — not simply to satisfy his own possessiveness, but to become someone who truly understood his partner. This time, it was that kind of relationship.

Liu had wanted to come here more often. It was Yeehyeon himself who had limited his visits to once a month, vowing not to let this relationship tear down his routine or his life. Most of the time, it was bearable, but sometimes it was incredibly difficult.

"Even if you suddenly want to see me on a whim, I'll rush over right away."

There were times he wanted to ignore the rule he'd set for himself — once a month — and summon Liu, using that old promise as an excuse. It was all the more tempting because he knew Liu would fly over instantly if he simply said he wanted to see him. His relationship with Liu had made life here more vibrant than before, yet it was Liu's absence that left him feeling even lonelier. The physical ache of not being able to touch or hold him was, in some ways, far more potent and vivid than any abstract idea.

In reality, he dreamed of Liu more often than he talked to him about it. He also couldn't tell Liu that sometimes he would sit alone in this room, gazing blankly at the ring in its case.

Yeehyeon closed the lid of the case, unable even to put it back in its proper place before being wearily pulled into sleep. Liu's parting remark — dream of me — had been meant as a joke to make him laugh, but just for tonight, Yeehyeon wished that remark could become a spell.
