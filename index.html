Diamond Dust

Written by Kim David

Having lived as a beta his whole life, Seo Yeehyeon never imagined his path would cross with a golden Alpha like Liu Weikun — someone so far above his world, it felt like fate would never even bother. But one day, Weikun catches an impossible scent adrift in the air: the sweet, addictive pheromone of an Omega… coming from Yeehyeon. Stranger still, it’s a scent only he can perceive. As desire and instinct blur into obsession, Yeehyeon finds himself caught between disbelief and temptation, his world crystallizing into one he never thought possible…
