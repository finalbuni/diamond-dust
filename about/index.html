Hello you ♡

I’m Demi~

Diamond Dust is the first English edition I’ve worked on. I started it because I loved the story and I couldn’t find a decent translation out there. After paying for chapters with a bunch of translation mistakes, I said fuck it and created my own version for myself. Then I realized I could share it with my fellow fujoshis who were running into the same problem. So I started putting together an English version I actually wanted to read, and this project was born.

Seeing you share the link, leave kind messages, and enjoy the story still gets me every single time. :')

If you’ve enjoyed your time here, leave me a note below! Your thoughts, feelings, favorite lines, anything at all. No pressure and no format. I read every single one.

If you’d like to help other readers find this English edition, you can also leave a review on NovelUpdates. And if you loved Diamond Dust, please consider supporting the original novel on Ridibooks. ♡

Thank you for being here. ♡

— finalbuni
