A new way to read chapters

Thank you to everyone who answered my reader survey! 💛

You can now choose how you want to read each chapter: the original full chapter or shorter sections.

Choose your reading format

Open any chapter and use the Read in dropdown beside the reading time to switch between formats.

Change your mind anytime

You can also switch formats in Reading Settings.

Short sections are divided at the chapter’s existing scene breaks, so their lengths vary. If you switch formats partway through a chapter, you’ll be taken to the beginning of the corresponding section.

And I HAVEN'T TOUCHED THE FULL CHAPTERS!! They're still available at their original links, so you can keep reading exactly as before. :)
