What’s new ♡

September 2026

You asked, I listened! Thank you to everyone who answered my reader survey. Some of you enjoy reading a whole chapter without interruptions, while others wanted smaller pieces with clearer places to stop. A few of you also mentioned wanting to load an entire chapter before going offline.

So I kept the original full-chapter experience and added another way to read: shorter sections. Each chapter is divided at its existing scene breaks. They aren’t all the same length, but you can read one section at a time and choose when to move to the next.

How to try it: Open any chapter as usual. Beside the reading time, choose Short sections from the reading-format selector. You can also switch formats in Reading Settings. If you switch while reading, the site takes you to the beginning of the corresponding section in the other format.

Full chapters are still here, with their existing links. You can keep reading exactly as before if that’s what you prefer. ♡

Thank you for helping me make this little corner of the internet better!
