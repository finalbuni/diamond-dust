<p>"Kun, Kun! Let's do spelling practice!"</p>

<p>The child ran to Liu, who was grabbing a slice of cake and some milk from the kitchen. Hugging his leg, the boy held a thin notebook in one hand.</p>

<p>"Spelling practice? Sure. Come sit at the table."</p>

<p>The child scrambled onto the chair on his knees.</p>

<p>The child's name was Leo — Liu and Ihyeon's neighbor. Born to a Korean chef father and an Australian painter mother, Leo had been born in Bali and grown up there ever since. Because of this, he was fluent in Korean, English, and even Balinese.</p>

<p>Since recently starting to learn to read, the child had become completely absorbed in spelling practice. Compared to the old days when Liu had to actively play with him, things had gotten a little easier.</p>

<p>Liu placed the cake and milk beside Leo's notebook and sat down across from him. The child raised his pencil high and shouted.</p>

<p>"Number one!"</p>

<p>"Okay, number one. What should we do for this one... Hmm. Cake."</p>

<p>The child, who had been watching Liu's lips with sparkling eyes, moved his pencil with a confident expression the moment the word was spoken. Resting his chin in his hand, Liu watched the child's small, glistening crown and smiled silently.</p>

<p>After their marriage, Liu and Ihyeon had moved, as planned, to Bali — Ubud, specifically. That had been five months ago already. Leo, who used to hide behind his parents' legs and couldn't even greet them properly when they first met, was now a close buddy to both of them.</p>

<p>"Cake!"</p>

<p>The child presented the crookedly written word to Liu.</p>

<p>"Correct! Leo, that's amazing. 'Cake' was a difficult word."</p>

<p>"It wasn't hard. Cake is easy."</p>

<p>"Here, since you got it right, have a bite of the cake too."</p>

<p>Liu took a piece of cake with a fork and placed it in the child's mouth. He was a little surprised by the boy, who was so focused on spelling practice despite having his favorite cake right in front of him.</p>

<p>"Okay, next is number two... What should I make number two?"</p>

<p>"Give me a hard word! Something hard!"</p>

<p>Wondering what word would be challenging enough yet still possible for Leo to get right, Liu looked around the room.</p>

<p>It was then that he heard a small voice begin to whimper and cry.</p>

<p>"Parang woke up!"</p>

<p>Leo was the first to shout it. Without needing to decide who would go first, both of them got up from their seats.</p>

<p>In the middle of the living room, on the mat and cushions, a small baby lay on his stomach, crying while flailing his arms and legs.</p>

<p>"Parang, did our Parang sleep well?"</p>

<p>Upon hearing Liu's voice, the baby's crying quieted. Liu lowered himself and revealed his face to the baby. Dark, wet eyes blinked as the baby examined him.</p>

<p>"You slept well and even managed to roll over by yourself?"</p>

<p>"Does Parang want to go swimming?"</p>

<p>Leo, who had come to Liu's side, asked while watching the baby with fascinated eyes.</p>

<p>The baby knew how to roll over but couldn't yet crawl. The sight of him lying on his stomach and waving his limbs around looked to Leo like he was trying to swim.</p>

<p>"No — he wants to crawl forward. He'll be scooting around everywhere soon. Right, Parang?"</p>

<p>As the baby's cries grew louder, Liu picked up the small body. Leo spoke in a worried voice.</p>

<p>"Parang must have been scared because no one was around."</p>

<p>"I suppose so. He must have been startled."</p>

<p>Liu soothed the baby with practiced ease. Usually the crying would have stopped right away, but this time the fussing didn't subside even with gentle rocking.</p>

<p>"Parang, what's upsetting you after waking up? Huh?"</p>

<p>While rocking the baby and nuzzling his nose against the fragrant little head, Liu checked the diaper. The yellow star on the outside had turned blue — the baby had peed.</p>

<p>"It was the diaper. I'll change it right away."</p>

<p>"The diaper? Should I go get the diaper?"</p>

<p>Leo asked, his eyes lighting up as though he'd been waiting for the chance. He always wanted to do something for the baby. Usually quite the wild one, Leo was extremely careful whenever he was around Parang.</p>

<p>"Would you?"</p>

<p>"You really love talking about poop, don't you, Leo?"</p>

<p>"It's funny."</p>

<p>"When Parang gets as old as Leo, will he also like talking about poop?"</p>

<p>"Of course!"</p>

<p>"Yep, I'm the hyung, so I have to protect Parang."</p>

<p>"I'll give Parang all my cake and all my toys!"</p>

<p>"Parang is the luckiest baby in all of Bali."</p>

<p>"Because of Leo?"</p>

<p>"Yes, because of you, Leo."</p>

<p>"Mom and Dad say that Kun taking care of the baby is like <em>ilhuilbi</em>."</p>

<p>"<em>Ilhuilbi</em>?"</p>

<p>At Leo's words, Liu's eyes widened as he asked back.</p>

<p>"Yeah, like getting better every day."</p>

<p>"Ah..."</p>

<p>Since he was still young and used multiple languages, Leo often made adorable mistakes that delighted the adults.</p>

<p>"So it means I've gotten better at taking care of Parang, right?"</p>

<p>"Yes!"</p>

<p>"Then what Mom and Dad said must have been <em>ilchwiyoljang</em>?"</p>

<p>"That's it! That's the word!"</p>

<p><em>Ilchwiyoljang, ilchwiyoljang</em>... Leo repeated the word a few times, then tilted his head and looked up at Liu.</p>

<p>"Then what is <em>ilhuilbi</em>?"</p>

<p>"Hmm, it's a slightly difficult word... <em>Ilhuilbi</em> is the feeling of being both happy and sad. It can also mean happy and sad things happening alternately. It might be a bit hard for you to understand, Leo."</p>

<p>"I know what that is."</p>

<p>"You do?"</p>

<p>"When I eat cake, I'm happy because it's delicious, but I'm also sad because it gets smaller."</p>

<p>"That's right. That is exactly <em>ilhuilbi</em>. You are really smart, Leo."</p>

<p>When Liu looked surprised and patted his head, Leo grinned proudly.</p>

<p>When it came time to pull up the pants after the diaper change, the baby started whining again. Liu soothed him while speeding up his movements.</p>

<p>"Our Parang got so bored. It's all done now, all done... finished!"</p>

<p>After pulling up the pants, Liu swept the baby up in a sudden embrace, and Parang giggled while kicking his little legs. His condition had clearly recovered after the diaper change. Liu adjusted his hold to make the baby comfortable and stood up.</p>

<p>The baby settled his head and neck steadily against Liu's left shoulder. He wrapped his chubby little arms around Liu's thick upper arm and gripped the hem of his T-shirt tightly. Liu turned his head, kissed the baby's head, and gently stroked his back with his large palm.</p>

<p>Leo tugged on the hem of Liu's pants and looked up.</p>

<p>"Parang looks happy when Kun holds him."</p>

<p>"Should I hold you too, Leo?"</p>

<p>"I'm not a baby."</p>

<p>Though he pretended to hesitate, Leo clearly wanted to be held too. He might have been deeply immersed in the role of a hyung lately, but he was still just a six-year-old boy. After scratching his lips with his finger as if thinking it over, Leo shrugged his small shoulders and arrived at a conclusion he could accept.</p>

<p>"Well, Kun always holds Ihyeon too, even though he's not a baby."</p>

<p>"......"</p>

<p>Liu was momentarily speechless with surprise.</p>

<p>It was interesting to see himself reflected through the child's words. He was always surprised that Leo noticed even the most ordinary aspects of their daily lives. Liu smiled and stroked the hair of the boy clinging to his leg.</p>

<p>"Ihyeon is special. Forever."</p>

<p>"Because you love him?"</p>

<p>"Yes, because I love him."</p>

<p>Still holding the baby in his left arm, Liu bent down and opened his right arm toward Leo. Conscious of the baby nestled against Liu's left side, Leo carefully climbed in. Holding both children in his arms, Liu returned to the kitchen and set Leo down on the dining chair.</p>

<p>The spelling session continued up to number ten, using words like "Parang," "love," and "thank you." Just as Leo was finishing the cake, the doorbell rang.</p>

<p>"Who could that be?"</p>

<p>Leo was the first to react happily to the visitor. Liu checked his wristwatch and lifted Parang high.</p>

<p>"Parang, looks like Daddy's here."</p>

<p>Even after promising marriage, Liu and Ihyeon couldn't move in together right away. There were still practical matters they needed to handle. But the fact that they had definitively promised marriage alone made the time they had to spend apart significantly less painful.</p>

<p>After their engagement, the two first came to Bali and signed the lease for their current house. The property, made up of two buildings, had appealed to them instantly. The building facing the road would be the gallery, and the one behind it was perfect as their home. They decided to renovate a small storeroom between the two buildings into Ihyeon's atelier.</p>

<p>Extensive remodeling took place before Liu and Ihyeon wrapped up their lives in Seoul and Paris, respectively, and moved to Bali. They even built a swimming pool in the courtyard. Getting the permit had caused some trouble, but it was a decision they would be satisfied with for years to come.</p>

<p>A separate main gate was installed between the gallery and the residence to ensure privacy. While interactions with affiliated artists or neighbors were always welcome — and the gallery had indeed become like a neighborhood living room — Liu wanted the residence to remain a purely private space for him and Ihyeon. Anyone could enter the gallery during business hours, but the main gate leading to the house could not be passed by just anyone.</p>

<p>The person who rang the doorbell at that gate and appeared was Seo Yeehan.</p>

<p>"Parang! Did you come out to greet Daddy?"</p>

<p>Yeehan's face filled with emotion the moment he saw the baby held by Liu, who had come out to the entrance.</p>

<p>"Daddy got even darker than this morning. Right, Parang?"</p>

<p>Liu turned his body so the baby in his arms could be seen more clearly by Yeehan.</p>

<p>"Tell me about it. I wondered if he could get any darker, but apparently it's possible."</p>

<p>Yeehan, dressed in a sleeveless shirt and shorts, looked so dark that his skin seemed close to a deep gray rather than just brown.</p>

<p>"Don't stand in the doorway; come in. We need to get Parang's things too. Can I get you some tea?"</p>

<p>"Ah, I'm so exhausted right now... hyung, give me coffee instead."</p>

<p>Indeed, Yeehan looked extremely tired — not surprising after teaching classes all day. As he took off his shoes and stepped inside, he looked down at Leo, who was clinging to Liu's leg, and smiled brightly.</p>

<p>"Hi, Leo."</p>

<p>"Hello."</p>

<p>Leo greeted him hesitantly.</p>

<p>"Did Leo help out a lot watching Parang?"</p>

<p>While Leo addressed Liu and Ihyeon familiarly by name, he treated Yeehan more formally, in the Korean way.</p>

<p>"I fanned Parang."</p>

<p>"Did you? Thank you so much. Please continue to look after our Parang for us."</p>

<p>Parang was the son Yeehan and Morae had obtained with great difficulty.</p>

<p>The male Beta and female Alpha had almost given up on having a child after trying for so long. It was Liu and Ihyeon who had introduced Marcus to them. Since Marcus and Ellen, who were in the same situation, had succeeded in having their son Jonas, Liu and Ihyeon thought they might be able to help.</p>

<p>They soon began tests to see if they could truly have a baby, and the results brought both joy and sorrow.</p>

<p>Fortunately, Morae also had mature eggs, just like Ellen. However, her uterus was not very developed. The doctor recommended retrieving Morae's eggs and using a surrogate as the primary option.</p>

<p>After much deliberation, the two decided to go with that method.</p>

<p>The immense cost of the entire, far-from-simple process was borne by Mr. Lim, Morae's father.</p>

<p>The very person who had long opposed their relationship — the one who had driven them to flee all the way to Bali — was now willing to cover the costs for their future child. It was an outstretched hand of reconciliation.</p>

<p>The surrogacy procedure was performed in Australia, where it was legal and close to Bali. Mr. Lim traveled to Australia himself to hold his grandson immediately after the birth. Now he was an ordinary grandfather, living with the satisfaction of watching his granddaughter by the East Sea and his grandson in Bali grow up — though he still occasionally insisted on things stubbornly, leaving his children stunned.</p>

<p>In any case, Parang was a miraculous baby who had come to the two of them after such a difficult wait.</p>

<p>The baby's name is Parang. Wave.</p>

<p>Ripples and big waves. Waves. It was a naming choice befitting surfer parents.</p>

<p>As they moved toward the kitchen, Parang, held in Liu's arms, stared intently at Yeehan. Then, apparently recognizing him, the baby stretched both arms out toward his father.</p>

<p>"Parang, do you recognize your dad? Do you want to come to Daddy?"</p>

<p>Overcome with emotion, Yeehan took the baby and held him close.</p>

<p>"I thought I would die from missing you."</p>

<p>Watching Yeehan gently kiss the baby's head, Liu felt a complicated mix of emotions.</p>

<p>"He cried whenever I was out of sight for even a moment. He kept telling me to hold him constantly."</p>

<p>"Really? That must have been tough for you, hyung."</p>

<p>"He was stuck to me all day, so seeing him run right to you makes me feel a little left out."</p>

<p>As he patted the diapered bottom of Parang nestled in his father's arms, Liu managed a bittersweet smile.</p>

<p>"You can become a father too, hyung."</p>

<p>At Yeehan's suggestion, Liu only offered an unreadable smile. He turned away, using the excuse of needing to prepare the coffee maker.</p>

<p>"Sit down, I'll make coffee in a moment. How was the lesson? Did everything go smoothly?"</p>

<p>"Thanks to you. Morae also asked me to pass along her thanks."</p>

<p>Yeehan and Morae ran a surfing school at Kuta Beach, one of Bali's famous surfing spots. Because they had other instructors, the owners didn't schedule lessons on weekends when the nanny had time off. However, this weekend there was a special advanced lesson they needed to attend. That's why they had entrusted the baby here.</p>

<p>Kuta was not a short distance from Ubud — even when traffic was good, it took an hour and a half. But whenever they were asked, Liu and Ihyeon gladly helped look after Parang.</p>

<p>This was only the second time they had brought Parang to Ubud instead of going to Kuta.</p>

<p>"It's Sunday, and Ihyeon is still working? The light was on in the atelier."</p>

<p>"He'll wrap things up and come over soon."</p>

<p>Liu glanced at his wristwatch and replied with a faint smile at the corner of his lips.</p>

<p>"His solo exhibition is about a month away now, right? He must be busy."</p>

<p>"He's always been the type to work diligently every single day. He isn't behind on his work, but since this is his first exhibition since coming here, I think he wants to do even better. He hasn't left the atelier all day."</p>

<p>Leaning against the counter, Liu looked worriedly toward the window facing the atelier. Except for a brief stop home for lunch, Ihyeon had been holed up in his studio for eight hours already.</p>

<p>"So you brought Parang home today because Ihyeon had to be in the atelier."</p>

<p>"I couldn't just leave Ihyeon alone and leave the house."</p>

<p>Yeehan let out a short laugh watching Liu shrug as if it were the most natural thing in the world.</p>

<p>"Is Ihyeon your baby, hyung?"</p>

<p>Liu, looking a bit awkward, just smiled without answering as he placed the filter into the dripper.</p>

<p>"Funny... you still light up just hearing Ihyeon's name, don't you?"</p>

<p>"Still? We're in the middle of our honeymoon phase, you know?"</p>

<p>"Ah, right. Of course. Is there anyone in Bali who <em>doesn't</em> know you two are newlyweds?"</p>

<p>He added the murmur, "Look how obvious you are," as if to himself, but it was clearly meant to be heard.</p>

<p>"I know too! Kun and Ihyeon are newlyweds!"</p>

<p>Leo, sitting next to Yeehan, raised his arm and shouted proudly, leaving Liu and Yeehan no choice but to laugh.</p>

<p>"Hyung's been watching the baby all day by himself because of Ihyeon's work?"</p>

<p>"Leo helped watch him."</p>

<p>"Even though he's my own son, watching him alone is really tough."</p>

<p>"What's 'tough'?"</p>

<p>Leo looked up at Yeehan, his eyes sparkling. Yeehan stroked the boy's head with a look of regret.</p>

<p>"Oh... no, I misspoke. I'm sorry."</p>

<p>While Yeehan was sweating over Leo, Liu brewed drip coffee with hot water. After pouring two cups and setting them on the table, his phone rang from the living room.</p>

<p>"I'll go get it!"</p>

<p>Recognizing the ringtone, Leo quickly slid off his chair and ran to the living room. He returned in an instant wearing a proud expression, happy to have helped. Liu patted the boy's head.</p>

<p>"Thanks, Leo. Oh? It's your dad."</p>

<p>The caller ID showed Leo's father.</p>

<p>"No! I'm not ready to go yet! I don't want to leave."</p>

<p>Sensing it was a call to come home, Leo leaned against the table, rubbed his face, and made a sour expression. Liu stroked the boy's hair and answered. Sure enough, it was a message to return home right away.</p>

<p>"Dad says to stop playing and come to the restaurant."</p>

<p>Leo's father ran a fusion Korean restaurant three minutes away, and the family lived on the third floor of the same building.</p>

<p>"Aww, but I want to play with Parang more."</p>

<p>Having grown quite attached to the baby, Leo went to Yeehan's side and fiddled with Parang's small foot.</p>

<p>"He said he bought you a bicycle. I heard you wanted one."</p>

<p>At the mention of a bicycle, the boy instantly changed his attitude completely. His eyes, sparkling with delight, quickly turned toward Liu.</p>

<p>"A bicycle? Awesome!"</p>

<p>Leo, who had been halfway to the front door, stopped and turned back. He stood holding the edge of the table and looked up at Yeehan, his expression decidedly polite.</p>

<p>"Mister, can I give Parang a goodbye kiss?"</p>

<p>"Of course, absolutely."</p>

<p>Leo carefully kissed the baby's forehead, then properly greeted both Kun and Yeehan before running back toward the front door.</p>

<p>Yeehan watched Leo's retreating back with a smile, then brought his mug to his lips and asked.</p>

<p>"'Awesome,' huh? Where did he pick up language like that?"</p>

<p>"I think he learned it from Yuni."</p>

<p>Baek Yuni had also left The Hands with Ihyeon and moved to Bali. More capable now with accumulated experience, she was in charge of the overall operation of the local gallery, alongside her partner, Michelle.</p>

<p>Michelle, who had worked as a designer in Paris, had joined Liu's team and handled all the visual media design for the gallery.</p>

<p>Jungles, rivers, the sea, rice paddies, lakes... Deeply immersed in Bali's diverse nature, Yuni and Michelle were thoroughly enjoying life there, working passionately on weekdays and taking short trips on weekends.</p>

<p>"But you must have had such a hard time taking care of the baby all by yourself. I'm so grateful."</p>

<p>"You said you'd treat me to a meal, right? Make it an expensive one."</p>

<p>"I definitely will. I'll buy good ingredients and cook a feast for you myself."</p>

<p>"I was just saying that. Taking care of Parang isn't that hard. How could it be hard when he's this cute, right, Parang?"</p>

<p>As Liu spoke to him from across the table, the baby made eye contact and giggled happily.</p>

<p>"You're surprisingly talented at childcare. Even the way you hold him is so steady. You don't look like someone who just babysits once in a while."</p>

<p>"I never really had the chance to spend time with kids before. It's kind of surprising to me, too."</p>

<p>"At first, you didn't know what to do when he was handed to you."</p>

<p>"He was so small — I was afraid I might hurt him."</p>

<p>"Now, when you take Parang out, everyone assumes you're his father, right?"</p>

<p>"I didn't know I could be like this either. Parang is precious. Maybe it's because he follows me so well? When he grips my shirt tightly with his little hands, it warms my heart."</p>

<p>Liu looked warmly at the baby, who was playing while tapping the table with his small hands in his father's arms. Yeehan stroked the baby's soft hair while looking at Liu.</p>

<p>"If it were your own child, wouldn't it be even more beautiful?"</p>

<p>"......"</p>

<p>Yeehan's voice held a subtle weight — this wasn't a casual remark. Because of that, Liu couldn't just laugh it off. Yeehan looked at Liu, who was only sipping his coffee, and asked as if he had made up his mind.</p>

<p>"You and Ihyeon aren't thinking about it yet?"</p>

<p>"Ihyeon is only twenty-six. And we haven't even been married for six months."</p>

<p>"Even if Ihyeon is only twenty-six, you're thirty-six."</p>

<p>"These days, thirty-six is just about the prime age for marriage. Look around me — Manager Han, Choi Inwu, Shushu. They're all my age, and I'm the only one who's married."</p>

<p>Liu said this with his characteristic confidence, tinged with a smile. Yeehan paused to think for a moment before nodding.</p>

<p>"Well, that's true."</p>

<p>"Ah, I should prepare Parang's dishes in advance. Our angel ate his baby food so well today. Right, Parang?"</p>

<p>Lifting himself off the chair, Liu stroked the baby's round cheek. The little one looked up at Liu, drooling onto his bib and grinning widely, kicking his arms and legs. Facing that sight, Liu could never help but smile back.</p>

<p>Weekends were the housekeeper's days off. Liu picked up the baby's dishes, which he had washed himself, from the drying rack. They were still a bit damp.</p>

<p>"Still, you know Ihyeon wants one, right?"</p>

<p>"......"</p>

<p>Liu's hands, wiping the dishes dry with a kitchen towel, paused momentarily. Without turning his head, he replied in a casual tone.</p>

<p>"That's a topic we can think about in a year or two. We can still enjoy being newlyweds, just the two of us, for a little longer, can't we?"</p>

<p>"It's a prejudice to think that having a baby means the newlywed atmosphere disappears. Can't you tell by looking at Morae and me?"</p>

It wasn't just camaraderie from raising a child together — they seemed more grateful for each other's existence, and felt a different kind of attraction in the way they cared for one another.</p>

<p>Even if they hired a trustworthy full-time babysitter, it wouldn't mean the parents' touch was entirely unnecessary. Even wearing a T-shirt with drool on his shoulder and his neckline stretched out from the baby pulling at it, Ihyeon would still be attractive. <p>"Morae said it, actually. That I was sexiest when I was kissing that tiny little head while holding Parang as I rocked him to sleep."</p>

<p>"Yes, of course. I'm sure she would."</p>

<p>As Liu packed the dried dishes into a special bag decorated with a bear, he chuckled softly.</p>

<p>"I guarantee it — when you see Ihyeon and the baby sleeping side by side, you might even cry from happiness, hyung."</p>

<p>"......"</p>

<p>This time, Liu couldn't say anything. Yeehan's words had forced him to imagine it.</p>

<p>Ihyeon and their baby. Ihyeon sleeping alongside that baby.</p>

<p>There could be no greater happiness. At least, not among the happiness he could imagine right now. Even without seeing it before his eyes, the sheer emotion overwhelmed him to the point of pain just by imagining it.</p>

<p>"I don't think having a child is necessary for love to be complete. But you aren't planning on never having any children, are you?"</p>

<p>"Your thoughts are pretty obvious, hyung."</p>

<p>The affectionate teasing from his friends rained down on Liu.</p>

<p>Yeehan narrowed his eyes, gazing into the air as he tried to recall something, then tilted his head.</p>

<p>"Ihyeon is only twenty-six, and we haven't even been married six months. Who was it that so firmly said it was too soon to have a baby… who was that again?"</p>

<p>Liu nodded as if admitting guilt, raising his left hand.</p>

<p>"Yes, that was me. I did say that."</p>

<p>"And yet, the moment he found out about the pregnancy, his grin stretched from ear to ear..."</p>

<p>"Besides, he didn't confess it to us first; we found out by accident."</p>

<p>Liu, who had flown straight from Hong Kong to Bali the moment he heard the news of the pregnancy, did not tell Yuni or the other staff members even after returning to Hong Kong the next day. They only knew that Ihyeon had been feeling unwell.</p>

<p>Without telling anyone — not his parents in Hong Kong, not Ihyeon's family by the East Sea, not his friends — Liu held onto that happiness as his own secret for a month.</p>

<p>He felt that such great happiness might invite envy.</p>

<p>It was a happiness too precious to dare speak of carelessly.</p>

<p>It was the kind of happiness that wasn't meant to be shown off while wearing it, but rather kept deep inside a drawer, only to be secretly taken out late at night and gazed upon with satisfaction. It was just like how Ihyeon treated the diamond necklace Liu had given him during his proposal.</p>

<p>However, because the happiness was so immense, it was also inevitable that it would eventually be discovered.</p>

<p>It happened because Liu overreacted when he found Ihyeon helping move artwork in the gallery ahead of his solo exhibition.</p>

<p>"That was the first time I'd ever seen the Director act so frighteningly. Ihyeon isn't a gallery employee; he's an affiliated artist. Why would you make him do that kind of work?"</p>

<p>Yuni added, setting down her glass after finishing the last bit of wine.</p>

<p>"It's a good thing we knew Ihyeon was pregnant; otherwise I really might have quit right then and there."</p>

<p>Even without having seen the situation, his friends knew exactly what had happened and chimed in about Liu's overprotectiveness.</p>

<p>Liu willingly played the sacrificial lamb, appearing completely unbothered as if inviting them to tease him all they wanted. No amount of teasing about Ihyeon and the baby could possibly put even a small dent in his happiness. Moreover, the teasing from everyone gathered there was rooted in strong affection, so there was no reason to feel displeased.</p>

<p>It seemed the meal was entirely finished.</p>

<p>Some time had passed since everyone had put down their food, and the wine was nearly gone as well.</p>

<p>Ihyeon, the host, and Liu exchanged a meaningful glance. It was time to bring out dessert.</p>

<p>As Ihyeon stood up first, followed immediately by Liu, Yeehan pointed at Liu as if he had been waiting for this moment.</p>

<p>"Look at that, look! See how he scurries right after Ihyeon as soon as he gets up!"</p>

<p>Liu turned around and threatened playfully.</p>

<p>"I'm just going to get the dessert. The carrot cake that your wife loves."</p>

<p>"Does only Morae like carrot cake? Everyone here knows that Ihyeon will wake up even from a dead sleep for Omia's carrot cake."</p>

<p>That was true. Liu feigned ignorance, shrugged his shoulders, and hurried into the kitchen after Ihyeon.</p>

<p>"There's probably no one to whom the word 'scurrying' is less fitting."</p>

<p>A burst of cheerful laughter erupted from behind the two of them at someone's remark. It was a lively sound they hadn't heard in the house for a long time.</p>

<p>There was a slight distance between the dining area, set up with a large communal table, and the kitchen. Despite that, the guests' jokes and laughter carried vividly into the kitchen. Thanks to that, Liu and Ihyeon couldn't help but smirk the entire time they were preparing the dessert.</p>

<p>Ihyeon placed the large whole cake onto the cake stand, and Liu took out dessert plates for the number of guests from the cabinet. Liu approached the counter with the plates, patted Ihyeon's shoulder, and asked kindly,</p>

<p>"Are you sleepy? Not tired?"</p>

<p>"A little sleepy, yes, but I'm okay for now. It's fun because everyone is gathered after a long time. I want to socialize a bit longer."</p>

<p>"That's a relief then."</p>

<p>Gently pulling Ihyeon closer into an embrace, Liu kissed the center of his mate's temple. Then, with a slightly flushed expression, he asked Ihyeon.</p>

<p>"Shall we bring out 'that' now?"</p>

<p>Ihyeon nodded with a smile, and Liu brought out a bundle from the pantry connected to the kitchen. It was a shopping basket containing several small paper boxes.</p>

<p>"So when are you announcing it? I'm dying of curiosity!"</p>

<p>Someone from the dining area called out loudly to the hosts, full of playful mischief.</p>

<p>Liu and Ihyeon, who were transferring the boxes onto a large tray, looked at each other and grinned.</p>

<p>When Liu appeared holding the cake and plates, and Ihyeon holding the tray with the boxes, all the guests' attention immediately focused on them.</p>

<p>"Ooh, what is that?"</p>

<p>"Are those presents?"</p>

<p>The reaction was good.</p>

<p>Cream-colored boxes tied with cream-colored ribbons were placed in front of each guest. Cream white was the color the two hosts had chosen for their wedding attire.</p>

<p>Some guests picked up the boxes and shook them, while others held them up high to catch the light. Leo, the only young guest, was the first to untie the ribbon and open his box.</p>

<p>"Balloons! Dad, they're balloons!"</p>

<p>As soon as Leo shouted excitedly, Liu immediately gave advice to the boy's father.</p>

<p>"Be careful, Leo, because you have to pierce it with a pin."</p>

<p>Led by Leo, the other guests also opened their boxes.</p>

<p>A small balloon inside the paper box and a small pin attached to the box to pop the balloon. Morae and Yeehan were the first to figure out its purpose. After all, they had also thrown a gender reveal party for Parang's surrogate mother.</p>

<p>"You said you weren't doing a separate gender reveal — when did you prepare something like this?"</p>

<p>"Because we said we'd throw a party for you."</p>

<p>"No, nothing that elaborate."</p>

<p>Ihyeon, standing next to Liu in front of the table, smiled faintly. Being the focus of a party was still something Ihyeon felt slightly awkward about.</p>

<p>"You invited us for dinner, but you two were making this all afternoon?"</p>

<p>"You two are really adorable."</p>

<p>The other guests held a box in their hands, enjoying themselves like children.</p>

<p>"What is it? How do we do this?"</p>

<p>Michelle kindly explained to Yuni, who had no idea how a gender reveal usually proceeded, standing next to her.</p>

<p>"It says right here on the box. Purple for a girl, yellow for a boy."</p>

<p>Leo's father, who had the boy sitting on his lap, also explained it to Leo, who was showing interest in the balloon.</p>

<p>"Leo, it's about Kun and Ihyeon's baby. If it's a little sister, purple paper will come out of the balloon, and if it's a little brother, yellow paper will come out."</p>

<p>Liu, leaning over while gripping the edge of the dining table with both hands, made eye contact with Leo and asked,</p>

<p>"Leo, would you prefer purple to come out, or yellow?"</p>

<p>"Purple!"</p>

<p>The child shouted decisively, raising his arms high without even hesitating.</p>

<p>"Purple? Why?"</p>

<p>"Parang and I are both boys, so I think it would be nice to have a little sister to play with."</p>

<p>Hearing Leo's answer, Liu and Ihyeon secretly exchanged glances and smiles that only they could understand.</p>

<p>"Leo, when I say one, two, three, we all pop them together, okay?"</p>

<p>One, two, three.</p>

<p>Pop, pop-pop, pop.</p>

<p>Someone failed to pierce it cleanly on the first try, and the air just hissed out. But most of them popped the balloons almost simultaneously with only a very slight delay.</p>

<p>A quiet stillness settled over the room for a brief moment.</p>

<p>What scattered across the table and the floor when the balloons burst was purple confetti.</p>

<p>"Ihyeon, is it a girl?"</p>

<p>The first to break the silence was Morae's flushed voice. Ihyeon nodded with a smile.</p>

<p>"Yes, nuna. Our Didi is a girl."</p>

<p>"How wonderful. What a beautiful baby she must be!"</p>

<p>Morae rose from her seat and approached Ihyeon. Not just her, but all the other guests followed suit and stood up. They congratulated and blessed the couple, exchanging hugs or kissing their cheeks.</p>

<p>"Daughter-obsessed Liu Weikun — that's easy to predict, isn't it?"</p>

<p>When Yeehan jokingly nudged Liu's arm, the other guests chimed in with their own comments.</p>

<p>"Are you going to carry her around constantly, never letting her walk on her own two feet?"</p>

<p>"How will you ever let her get married, she's too precious!"</p>

<p>Liu shrugged, wrinkling the bridge of his nose.</p>

<p>"We're already talking about marriage? What about all the steps in between?"</p>

<p>Yuni and Michelle gathered the confetti that had fallen onto the table and sprinkled it over Liu and Ihyeon's heads. Liu gently picked a piece of paper off Ihyeon's face and put on a playful front toward the guests.</p>

<p>"Alright, now that we know Didi's gender, everyone go home. Ihyeon is tired."</p>

<p>"We've only just had one bite of cake. Are you kicking us out?"</p>

<p>Yeehan held up his fork, looking aggrieved. Yuni scooped a piece of cake onto a plate and took over what Yeehan had started saying.</p>

<p>"They say that anything a husband does that disappoints you during pregnancy stays with you forever. But when I asked Ihyeon, he said he didn't feel even a speck of disappointment."</p>

<p>"It makes sense he wouldn't. He's been so devoted."</p>

<p>"But aren't <em>we</em> disappointed? He's been so cold to us ever since Ihyeon got pregnant."</p>

<p>Moonlight streamed in through the large window next to the dining table. The purple confetti scattered around the table twinkled as if coming to life in the moonlight.</p>

<p>By the time he saw the guests out to the front gate and returned, it was past eleven at night. It had been quite a while since Ihyeon had stayed up this late.</p>

<p>As soon as he closed the front door and they were alone, Liu gently wrapped his arms around Ihyeon's back and stroked his hair. Ihyeon looked a little tired.</p>

<p>"Aren't you tired?"</p>

<p>"A little."</p>

<p>"Go wash up first."</p>

<p>"What about Kun?"</p>

<p>"I'll just bring the plates to the kitchen and come in. Seo Ihyeon should lie down quickly."</p>

<p>Even though the cleaning lady would handle the dishes and tidying up the next day, it was Liu's habit to at least move the plates himself.</p>

<p>"I'll help you too."</p>

<p>"We agreed not to feel bad about things like this."</p>

<p>Ihyeon, who had been stroking Liu's arm, nodded after a brief pause.</p>

<p>"Then, I'll leave it to you."</p>

<p>Liu watched Ihyeon walk down the hallway toward the bedroom, let out a soft "Hmm," and then headed to the dining room.</p>

<p>The dining table where seven people, including Leo, had eaten and drunk was quite messy. After moving the dishes and roughly sweeping up the confetti that had fallen on the floor and table, quite a bit of time had passed.</p>

<p>He turned off the lights in the dining room and kitchen and secured the front door one last time. After drawing the curtains on the living room window completely, he turned off the living room light just before stepping into the hallway.</p>

<p>As everything went dark, the faint light seeping from the end of the hallway felt incredibly bright.</p>

<p>Warm light spilled out from the gap in the bedroom door, which was open about a hand's width. Liu smiled at that light, as if it were a signal that Ihyeon was waiting for him.</p>

<p>Just as Liu expected, Ihyeon was asleep.</p>

<p>He was propped up against the headboard with a pillow, sitting that way.</p>

<p>He had fallen asleep waiting for Liu.</p>

<p>Liu first adjusted the light until it was dimmer. Then, sitting in the armchair next to the bed, he gazed at Ihyeon's sleeping figure for a moment.</p>

<p>The room was utterly quiet, making the boisterousness from just a moment ago feel like a distant memory. Only Ihyeon's regular breathing could be heard comfortingly.</p>

<p>Whether consciously or not, Ihyeon had his left hand resting gently on his stomach — as if protecting the belly that had just started to show a gentle curve. Liu carefully placed his own left hand over that one.</p>

<p>Beneath their joined hands, their baby was breathing healthily.</p>

<p>Liu sometimes couldn't believe that one human being could bestow this much happiness upon another. <em>Was I making Seo Ihyeon this happy? As happy as Seo Ihyeon makes me?</em></p>

<p>Probably not. It couldn't be. It felt like it couldn't possibly be this much happiness.</p>

<p>However, he was certain that he would bet everything — his entire body, heart, and life — on loving Ihyeon. Just as he had once promised, he would protect Ihyeon's choices and hopes with everything he had. And now, not just for Ihyeon, but for Ihyeon and the baby. For this miraculous being that Ihyeon was nurturing within his own body through sacrifice and endurance.</p>

<p>Yeehan had been right when he said that having a baby didn't mean the honeymoon was over.</p>

<p>Because life with Ihyeon would be a honeymoon every day.</p>

<p>The matching rings on their joined hands shone softly, like moonlight.</p>

<p>As Liu himself had once sharply observed, even diamonds weren't forever. But he knew this light — the light emitted by his own Diamond Dust — would never fade.</p>

<p>It was a light that would illuminate the two of them as one, forever, or at least for a very long time, close to forever.</p>
